Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about travel bookings and job applications.

How do I apply for a job through SkyBridge Travels?

Browse our Jobs page, use the filters to find a role that matches your skills, click "Apply Now", and complete the online application form. You'll receive a reference number instantly. Our recruitment team reviews every application and contacts shortlisted candidates directly.

Are the job listings verified?

Yes. Every employer is vetted before we list their vacancies. However, we always recommend candidates independently verify company details and never pay upfront fees for a job offer. SkyBridge Travels never charges applicants for applying.

Do you help with visas and relocation?

We provide general guidance on relocation and can connect you with licensed immigration consultants in some countries. We do not process visas ourselves and cannot guarantee visa approval — that decision always rests with the destination country's authorities.

Can I cancel or change a tour booking?

Cancellation and change policies vary by package and are listed on each tour page. In general, cancellations made 30+ days before departure receive a full refund minus a small admin fee. Contact us directly for specific bookings.

Is SkyBridge Travels free for job seekers?

Absolutely. Job seekers never pay to browse listings, apply, or receive placement support. Employers pay a fee for verified listings, which keeps the platform free for candidates.

How do I become a partner employer?

Email info@skybridgesolutionsgh.com with your company details and the roles you'd like to list. Our team will verify your organisation and set you up with an employer account.
