Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 January 2025

SkyBridge Travels ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, how we use it, and the rights you have over it. It applies to our website, mobile interfaces, and any related services.

1. Who We Are

SkyBridge Travels is a travel and international recruitment platform. Our registered office is at Ho, Central Market Police Station, Building, Ghana. For privacy-related questions, you can reach us at info@skybridgesolutionsgh.com.

2. Personal Data We Collect

Depending on how you use our platform, we may collect:

  • Identity data: full name, date of birth, nationality, passport number (for travel bookings).
  • Contact data: email address, phone number, postal address.
  • Application data: CV/resume, cover letter, qualifications, work history, references.
  • Booking data: travel dates, destinations, dietary and accessibility requirements.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser type, device information, pages visited.
  • Usage data: search queries, filters applied, and interactions with job listings.
  • Communications: messages you send us via forms, email, or support channels.

3. How We Use Your Data

We process your personal data to:

  • Match you with relevant job opportunities and forward your application to the employer.
  • Process tour bookings, confirm availability, and coordinate travel logistics.
  • Respond to enquiries and provide customer support.
  • Send transactional emails (booking confirmations, application receipts).
  • Send marketing emails if you have opted in (you can unsubscribe at any time).
  • Detect and prevent fraud, spam, and abuse of our platform.
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms & Conditions.

4. Legal Basis for Processing

Where GDPR or similar laws apply, we rely on the following legal bases: contract (to fulfil bookings and applications), legitimate interests (to operate and improve our platform), consent (for marketing and optional cookies), and legal obligation (for tax, accounting, and compliance).

5. Sharing Your Data

We share your data only when necessary:

  • Employers: when you apply for a job, your application (including CV) is forwarded to the listed employer.
  • Tour operators and travel partners: to fulfil your booking.
  • Service providers: hosting, email delivery, analytics, payment processors — all under data-processing agreements.
  • Legal authorities: when required by law or to protect our rights.

We never sell your personal data to third parties.

6. International Transfers

Because we operate globally, your data may be transferred to and processed in countries outside your own. Where required, we use Standard Contractual Clauses and other approved safeguards to ensure an adequate level of protection.

7. Data Retention

We keep application data for up to 24 months after submission, unless you request earlier deletion. Booking records are retained for 7 years to meet tax and legal requirements. Marketing data is kept until you unsubscribe.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion ("right to be forgotten").
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Request data portability.
  • Withdraw consent at any time (where consent is the basis).
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

To exercise any of these rights, email info@skybridgesolutionsgh.com. We respond within 30 days.

9. Cookies & Tracking

We use essential cookies to keep the site functioning, and optional analytics cookies to understand how visitors use our platform. You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling essential cookies may affect site functionality.

10. Security

We use industry-standard safeguards including HTTPS encryption, hashed passwords, access controls, and regular security reviews. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure — you share data at your own risk.

11. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If you believe a minor has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the latest revision. Continued use of our platform after changes means you accept the updated policy.

13. Contact Us

Questions about this policy? Email info@skybridgesolutionsgh.com or write to us at the address in Section 1.

This document is provided as a template and does not constitute legal advice. Consult a qualified lawyer to tailor it to your jurisdiction and business.
